## Phase 1A — The authentic Nepali kundali format

### What we currently draw

North-Indian diamond (square outline, corner-to-corner diagonals, central
diamond): house 1 = top-centre diamond, houses counted **counterclockwise**
(2nd house top-left triangle … 12th top-right triangle), houses FIXED in
position, signs rotate with the Lagna. Cells show the sign name (written out),
the sign number (Nepali digits in NE mode), planet abbreviations (Devanagari in
NE mode), and the Lagna cell is highlighted and labelled लग्न / "Lagna".

### Verdict — stated plainly

**There is no distinct "Nepali" chart geometry.** The janma kundali used in
Nepal — in printed patris, in Hamro Patro, in Nepali jyotish practice and in
Nepali-built software — **is the North-Indian fixed-house diamond**: Lagna in
the top-centre diamond, houses counted counterclockwise, sign number written
in each cell. Our existing geometry was already correct.

What *does* differ in the Nepali presentation (and what a Nepali user
recognises as "our kundali"):

1. **Degrees written in the chart** — Mero Jyotish prints "Asc (28°)",
   "Sun 20°" inside the cells; we printed bare abbreviations. *(Gap — fixed in
   Phase 2: the Nepali style shows each planet's degree-in-sign and the Lagna
   degree.)*
2. **Devanagari everywhere in NE mode** — sign names, planet names, Nepali
   digits (we already did this).
3. **The china bundle around the chart** — a Nepali janma patri presents
   Lagna + Chandra + Navamsa charts together with the graha-spashta table,
   dasha balance and naming letter; the patri is as much the *document* as the
   diamond. (We show D1/D9/D10 + tables; Phase 2 adds the naming letter to the
   at-a-glance strip.)
4. **Dashas beyond Vimshottari** — Hamro Patro offers **Yogini** and
   **Trivagi (Tribhagi)** dashas, which Nepali users expect. We had only
   Vimshottari. *(Gap — fixed in Phase 2.)*

### Phase 2 chart decision (implemented)

- **Default style "Nepali (नेपाली)"** = the authentic diamond above, with
  in-cell degrees and full Nepali treatment. This is the default on load.
- **"North Indian"** selectable = the previous rendering (abbreviations, no
  degrees) — kept for users who prefer the compact software look.
- **"South Indian"** selectable = the fixed-sign square grid (signs fixed,
  Aries top row; Lagna sign marked), the other mainstream style Nepali
  software (Jyotish Sarathi) offers.

## Phase 3 — Premium report rebuild (2026-10-23, Samir's verdict: "premium report is very poor, it can be easily found in free websites")

### The API reality (stated to Samir, recorded here)
Parashara's Light and Jagannatha Hora expose no public API. The premium
sites sell finished reports, not APIs; the astrology APIs that do exist
(Prokerala and similar) are paid black boxes whose output is shallower
than what this engine already computes. There is no premium API to plug
in. What premium reports actually sell is **method depth plus
combinatorial interpretation**: every paragraph stitched from the
individual chart's facts (lordships carried, sign/house/nakshatra,
dignity, aspects both ways, strength scores), so no two reports read
alike. That — not an API — is what was built. No premium site's text was
copied; all methods are classical (BPHS / Jaimini / KP, public domain)
and all wording is original to this project, bilingual EN/नेपाली.

### Why v1 read "free" (diagnosis)
- `report.chapters()` emitted 3–4 one-line `_placement_fact` strings per
  life area ("X rules your Nth house; placed in the Mth house in Sign").
  No aspects, no conjunctions, no strength integration, no synthesis.
- `predictions.py` leaned on generic nature lines ("X is a natural
  benefic, so this period tends to bring support and growth") — the same
  sentence for every native running that planet's period.
- The 10-year forecast was one templated sentence per year from the MD
  lord alone; dasha-phala was placement line + nature line.
- No planet chapters existed at all; no aspects (graha drishti) were
  computed for interpretation; Ashtakavarga was a bare table; there were
  no special lagnas, no sign dasha, and transit reading was one
  good/bad line per planet.

### Engine additions (all computed, all tested)
- **Special lagnas** (`astro/jaimini.py`): Arudha Lagna + Upapada Lagna
  with the sutra exception (an arudha may not fall in the base sign or
  its 7th; take the 10th from the computed arudha — the JHora/PVR
  resolution, verified against PyJHora source), Chandra Lagna data, and
  readings (AL = public image, UL = spouse/marriage) from lord,
  occupants and aspecting planets. The Jaimini stronger-co-lord ladder
  (Mars/Ketu for Scorpio, Saturn/Rahu for Aquarius) is implemented in
  full: sign-occupation rule, conjunction count, Jupiter/Mercury/
  dispositor association via rasi drishti, exaltation, sign-modality
  strength, degree.
- **Jaimini Chara dasha — K. N. Rao's method** (`astro/chara.py`):
  sequence forward unless the 9th from Lagna is an even-footed
  (samapada) sign; durations = inclusive zodiacal count (sign→lord for
  odd-footed, lord→sign for even-footed) minus 1 (1→12), +1 for an
  exalted lord, −1 for a debilitated lord, dual lordships via the
  stronger co-lord. **Golden-verified two ways**: (a) reproduces
  P. V. R. Narasimha Rao's published worked example shipped in
  PyJHora's own test suite (Chennai 1996-12-07 10:34 → durations
  10/12/4/12/9/9/9/6/5/4/3/11) exactly; (b) the sample chart's twelve
  durations hand-computed. Antardashas of the running period included.
- **Transit-phala** (`astro/interpret.py` + `astro/transits.py`): each
  slow planet read from BOTH Lagna and Moon (house, classical gochar
  quality from the Moon, houses aspected in transit, next ingress date);
  **Sade Sati span with exact ingress dates** (bracketed by bisected
  sidereal ingresses — golden-checked against the documented
  2012-08-04 / 2014-11-02 / 2017-01-26 / 2020-01-24 Saturn ingresses for
  a Scorpio Moon); **double transit** — the signs jointly activated by
  Saturn's and Jupiter's transit aspects, listed as houses from Lagna
  and Moon.
- **Ashtakavarga interpretation**: per key house (1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10,
  11) the SAV band plus the house karaka's BAV, and the chart's
  strongest/weakest signs.

### Interpretation engine v2 (`astro/interpret.py`, ~1,130 lines)
Templated combinatorial generators; every paragraph cites ≥3 concrete
facts of the chart at hand (enforced by construction and by tests):
- **Planet chapters ×9** — functional role for this Lagna (lordships with
  classical role tags, Yogakaraka / benefic-leaning / malefic-leaning /
  maraka flags, natural significations), placement paragraph (house,
  sign, nakshatra-pada, full dignity incl. moolatrikona and
  friend/enemy dispositor relation, combustion, retrogression,
  conjunctions, dispositor routing), aspect paragraph (aspects cast and
  received, with the other planets' lordships named), Jaimini karaka
  identity, Navamsa corroboration (vargottama flagged), Shadbala rank +
  Ishta/Kashta tilt. Nodes get dispositor-based chapters.
- **House chapters ×12** — lord's placement/dignity chain, every occupant
  read in its lordship role, aspects received with roles, SAV + karaka
  BAV, karaka placement, Bhava Bala.
- **Divisional chapters** — D9 (vargottama list, D9 7th lord, Venus,
  Moon, Atmakaraka), D10 (10th-lord chain D1↔D10, Saturn, Sun), D7, D4,
  D12 (father/mother lords), D24, D20 — all from actual varga placements.
- **Dasha deep-dive** (replaces dasha_phala; same payload shape): each
  Mahadasha read through its lord's full role; all Antardashas of the
  running Mahadasha with dates and MD–AD interplay (positional relation,
  mutual aspect, natural friendship, jointly activated houses).
- **Timing synthesis**: current Vimshottari and Chara periods read
  together with an explicit agreement/echo/contrast analysis, plus the
  double transit as the third witness.
- **Forecast v2**: each year now also carries the running Chara sign and
  the year's real Saturn/Jupiter/Rahu ingresses interpreted from this
  chart's Moon sign (ingress chains computed by bisection).
- **Remedies**: in addition to the old picks, two-weakest-by-Shadbala
  support (mantra + rudraksha each) with the classical caution — no
  gemstone for a dusthana-lord weak planet.
